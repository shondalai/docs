You can build and teach a complete course with VidyaLMS Free. There is no learner limit imposed by the plugin, and WordPress Free does not need a license key. Pro is a separate add-on for sites that need more assessment tools, organization training or administrative workflows.

The feature split is the same on Joomla and WordPress. Their shop integrations differ: Joomla uses EasyCommerce and WordPress uses WooCommerce.

## Starting with Free

Install the main package for your platform, create a course, add lessons and choose how learners can enrol. You can teach a free course or connect it to a product in your shop. The shop handles payment methods, invoices, refunds and subscriptions; VidyaLMS handles learning access.

When a learner completes a course, Free can issue a certificate with a verification code. Learners can download the HTML certificate and print it using their browser. A visual certificate editor and PDF generation are Pro features.

## Adding Pro later

Install the matching Pro add-on alongside Free. Your courses, learners and progress stay in place. Pro adds its screens to the existing workspace; no separate learning site is needed. Connected services, including AI models and identity providers, still need configuration and may have their own charges.

If a guide describes an organization, automation, migration, AI or certificate-design screen that you cannot see, check that Pro is installed and that your account has permission to use it.
