VidyaLMS helps you turn what you know into courses people can follow, practise, and complete. Use it to teach an individual learner, run scheduled classes, or deliver training to several organizations from the same site.

These guides are for the people who run the academy, teach its courses, and support its learners. You do not need to be a developer to follow the everyday setup instructions.

## What would you like to do?

- **Make the academy feel like your site:** choose your [header, navigation, and appearance](configuration/learner-experience.md).
- **Build learning content:** create [modules and lessons](courses/lessons.md), [quizzes](courses/quizzes.md), and reusable [question banks](courses/question-banks.md).
- **Assess practical work:** collect several photos, use a rubric, request a revision, and unlock the next lesson after [assignment approval](courses/assignments.md).
- **Give someone access:** [enrol a learner](people/enrolments.md), [assign organization learning](people/organization-learning.md), or [sell a course](commerce/overview.md).
- **Keep learners informed:** configure [notifications](communication/notifications.md), write [email templates](communication/email-templates.md), and build [automations](communication/automations.md).
- **Recognize and review progress:** issue [certificates](credentials/certificates.md), explore [reports](reports/reports.md), and understand [active learning time](reports/learning-time.md).
- **Get something working again:** use the [troubleshooting guide](help/troubleshooting.md).

## How the pieces fit together

A **course** contains modules and lessons. A learner receives **access**, joins the course, and builds up progress. Your completion rules decide when the course is finished. A certificate can then recognize that achievement.

Your CMS continues to manage website accounts and sign-in. For paid courses, **EasyCommerce handles sales on Joomla** and **WooCommerce handles sales on WordPress**. VidyaLMS connects a successful purchase to learning access. Set prices, payment methods, subscriptions, invoices, and refunds in the shop.

## Why your screen may look different

The available controls depend on your permissions and the extensions installed on your site. Pro and Business features add assessment tools, organization services, report formats, and integrations. A feature listed as optional in these guides requires its supporting package or connected service; installing VidyaLMS alone does not configure that service.

The [feature reference](help/feature-reference.md) points to the right guide for each area and explains these dependencies. If a button is missing, check that reference before changing permissions or reinstalling anything.

New to the terminology? Keep the [plain-language glossary](help/glossary.md) nearby. Before inviting your first real learners, complete the [launch checklist](getting-started/launch-checklist.md).
